# Lessons

- Ask the question before generating the answer.
- No vote on swapping two players while a game is in progress or unscored.
- A vote is not a command. Blitz executes settled playbook.
- ESPN fantasy tag controls IR, not the NFL.com feed.
- Dropping an IR player frees IR, not a bench spot.
- Recon: do not say “only LY will claim Gordon” and also “GGT has two RBs.” GGT is the threat.
- Points-for playoffs: maximize projected points. Do not tank a week for a matchup.
