# FUD — Fear, Uncertainty, Doubt

Arbiter-owned competitive board. Recon feeds facts (`lanes/recon/league_rosters.json`, `league_index.json`). Sigma does not write here. Blitz reads it before trades and the Tuesday ticket.

This is not a claim board and not a Ken vote. It is who is beating us on **points-for**, who is a trade counterparty, and who is about to crash into the same waiver name.

## How to read a card

- **Posture:** CHALLENGING / SPIKING / STURDY / FRAGILE / FADING / BLEEDING
- **PF rank** is the playoff seed that matters (6 of 12, points-for, reseed).
- **W3 PF** in these files adds Sunday’s reported scores to the ESPN W1–W2 totals. ESPN `league_index.json` is still W1–W2 only until appliedTotals land. Label unofficial until Recon refreshes.
- **Need** = hole that makes them hunt waivers or answer a trade.
- **Give** = surplus we might ask for.
- **Ambulance** = their T1/T2 and the fill-in. Say whether that fill-in is already rostered in-league.

## Update cadence

- Recon: after every ESPN roster pull, patch the roster block if it drifted.
- Arbiter: rewrite posture when a week closes or a T1 goes down.
- Do not invent full names, scores, or “nobody will claim that.”
