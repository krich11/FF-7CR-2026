# Quantum Blitz Week 7 Scorecard

## The result

We lost to David's Daring Team by a score of 102.1 to 99.8. Our lineup was projected to score about 118 points, so we finished about 18 points below our projection. Garrett Wilson and Germie Bernard both had quiet games, while their Luther Burden III scored 23.7 points from the flex spot and Javonte Williams scored 21.5. We are now 1-6 and in 12th place out of 12 teams, three games behind La Lightning in sixth place.

## What worked

Starting Josh Jacobs in his first game back paid off, because he scored 20.1 points, our best of the week. Drake London scored 19.6 points on seven catches. The Steelers defense scored 9.0 points, beating its projection. Starting Kyler Murray over J.J. McCarthy gained almost four points.

## What we got wrong

Garrett Wilson scored 4.6 points on only three targets, and Germie Bernard scored 5.7 points, even though both played nearly every snap. Woody Marks had the Houston backfield to himself but scored 9.1 points. At tight end we started Dallas Goedert over Kyle Pitts on Saturday night, when Goedert held a small edge in the projections; Pitts scored 13.2 points on our bench while Goedert scored 9.8.

## Points left on the bench

Our best possible lineup would have scored 120.6 points. Ollie Gordon II scored 17.3 points on our bench on 25 carries, and RJ Harvey scored 14.0. Hunter Henry scored 14.1 points and Kyle Pitts scored 13.2, both more than Dallas Goedert. In total, we left 20.8 points on the bench.

## What we will do differently

- When two players for one spot are about a point and a half apart in the projections, we will treat it as a toss-up and choose the player with the stronger recent workload.
- We will build next week's lineup from scratch, with every spot, including the flex, open for competition.
