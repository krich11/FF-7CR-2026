# Quantum Blitz Week 6 Scorecard

## The result

We lost to Buck up Buttercup! by a score of 125.74 to 113.02. Our lineup was projected to score 104.41, so we beat our own projection by about nine points. Their Christian McCaffrey scored 30.7 points and their tight end Colston Loveland scored 18.5, and that was enough to beat us. We are now 1-5 and in 11th place out of 12 teams.

## What worked

Every move we made during the week paid off. Germie Bernard, whom we picked up Wednesday and started in the flex spot, scored 25.7 points, while TreVeyon Henderson, the player he replaced, scored 3.0 on our bench. Your Sunday morning switch from kicker Eddy Pineiro to Matt Gay gained seven points, because Gay scored 12.0 and Pineiro scored 5.0. Woody Marks, our waiver claim, scored 10.1 points as Houston's lead running back. RJ Harvey scored 14.6 points after we chose to start him on Thursday.

## What we got wrong

Jacoby Brissett scored only 6.92 points, which was the biggest disappointment of the week. Michael Penix Jr. was available as a free agent and scored 23.1 points, although the two quarterbacks had the same projection going in. Our weekly plan listed Eddy Pineiro as our only kicker for four days, even though our own research had found better kickers on Wednesday. You had to find Matt Gay yourself on Sunday morning. On Friday the Eagles announced that Jalen Hurts would not play, and we should have taken a fresh look at our tight end decision at that point. We also told you on Sunday that our team was projected to score about 124 points, when the correct figure was about 107.

## Points left on the bench

Our best possible lineup would have scored 131.42 points, which would have beaten Buck up Buttercup! by almost six points. Kyle Pitts scored 23.0 points on our bench, compared with 12.1 for Dallas Goedert at tight end. Hunter Henry scored 14.8 points on our bench and could have played the flex spot, with Bernard moving to wide receiver in place of Garrett Wilson, who scored 8.4. Jakobi Meyers scored 13.3 points on our bench, slightly more than Drake London's 12.2. In total, we left 18.40 points on the bench.

## What we will do differently

- Before we use a backup plan that was written earlier in the week, we will make sure it still fits our current roster.
- After every lineup change, we will update our record of the lineup so that it matches what is actually set on ESPN.
- When a starter's team changes quarterbacks, we will compare that starter with our bench players before kickoff.
- For kicker and defense, the weekly plan will always name the best available free agent, and if that player is projected to score at least 1.5 points more, we will ask you the same day whether to pick him up.
- Before we quote a team total, we will add up the actual player projections.
