## Method correction (one)
- **Close-call rule.** When two candidates for the same slot project within 1.5 PPR, the card does not pick on projection. It marks the slot CLOSE and picks on workload: expected touches/targets and snap share from the last two weeks (nflverse snaps now available), with a season-ending injury ahead of a player counting as a full role change. If Assistant or CONSULT dissents on a CLOSE slot, the card answers that dissent in one written line or takes the dissent. CLOSE slots are reopened at the Sunday 10:35 AM CT sweep. This fits the Week 5 Saturday-card law (every player, every close call, the inactive news that flips it).
