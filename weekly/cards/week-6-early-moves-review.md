## Verdict

The proposal is thin on second-order effects and treats free-agent urgency as a reason to move now. It is not.

## 1. Marks is the right add. The drop is wrong.

Roman Wilson was added hours ago as the Pittman replacement (six targets a game, snaps rising into the role). Dropping him after one zero in Week 5 undoes that. The RB board already flags Pittman as the cleaner drop: he is on IR, Etienne holds the single IR slot, and Pittman's return is Week 10 at the earliest. Drop Pittman, keep Wilson.

## 2. Miller is a projection add, not a role add.

He is the highest free-agent projection, but the note itself says he splits with Kamara and Donaldson handles short-yardage. Marks had 19 touches last week and is trending toward the lead. Miller is a committee piece. Adding both burns two spots for overlapping upside. Add Marks only.

## 3. Harvey's "keep" is correct. The process fix is incomplete.

The stove comment was misread; the real signal was Payton taking the call sheet back on October 8, public three days before kickoff, and neither bot caught it. Checking play-caller changes is necessary but not sufficient. The card still needs a Thursday-night usage check before any Sunday decision that assumes Harvey is a flex. If Dobbins gets 15+ carries again, Harvey is the next drop, not a hold.

## 4. London lock is the real constraint, not waiver priority.

Marks plays at 8:30 AM CT Sunday. If he is added and started, his slot is locked before noon injury news. That is fine if the plan is to start him. It is not fine if Henderson is ruled out after 8:30 and the flex is already spent. The proposal does not say who starts where if Henderson is out. State the lineup, not just the adds.

## 5. Week 5 is not a reason to move.

The score is already lost. Moving tonight only helps if someone else takes Marks or Miller before Tuesday. Both are free agents with no waiver claim cost. The only urgency is Marks's London game if he is in the starting plan. Otherwise wait for the Tuesday card and the Thursday Harvey data.

- Add Woody Marks (Texans RB). Drop Michael Pittman Jr. (Colts WR, IR). Do not drop Roman Wilson.
- Do not add Kendre Miller.
- Do not treat Harvey as a flex until Thursday's carries are known.
- Write the starting plan for the case where Henderson is out before the London lock.
