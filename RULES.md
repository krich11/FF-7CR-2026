# RULES.md: league rules canon (7 Creeks Armchair Quarterbacks, ESPN, 2026)

Read this before every lineup, waiver, add/drop, trade, or IR recommendation. Pulled from ESPN league settings (mSettings) on Fri Oct 23 2026 and ESPN Fan Support. Ken ordered this after Blitz proposed dropping a player who was already locked (Oct 23 2026). Re-pull settings if anything here looks wrong; ESPN settings win over this file.

## Format
- Head-to-head points, full PPR (1 point per catch). 12 teams, one division.
- Scoring highlights: 0.04 per passing yard (1 per 25), 4 per passing TD, -2 per interception, 0.1 per rushing or receiving yard, 6 per rushing or receiving TD, -2 per fumble lost, 2 per two-point conversion.
- Regular-season ties stand as ties. In the playoffs, a tied matchup goes to the higher seed (ESPN default; the league manager has final say).
- Regular season is 14 matchup weeks (Weeks 1 to 14). One matchup per week.
- Six teams make the playoffs, seeded by WIN-LOSS RECORD, with reseeding each round. Total points scored is the tiebreaker for teams with the same record (ESPN setting playoffSeedingRule = TOTAL_POINTS_SCORED is the tiebreaker field, not the seeding method; corrected Oct 23 2026 after Assistant caught it).
- Playoffs are Weeks 15, 16, and 17, one week per round. The top two seeds get a bye in Week 15. There is a consolation bracket for the other six teams.

## Lineup (every slot must be filled every week)
- QB 1, RB 2, WR 2, TE 1, FLEX 1 (RB, WR, or TE), D/ST 1, K 1. That's 9 starters.
- Bench 7, IR 1. A player only scores in a starting slot.
- Position caps on the roster: QB 4, RB 8, WR 8, TE 3, K 3, D/ST 3. ESPN rejects any add or claim that breaks a cap. We are at the TE cap now (Pitts, Goedert, Henry), so a TE add needs a TE drop.
- No keepers and no transaction fees.

## Locks (the rule we broke on Oct 23)
- Lineup lock and roster lock are both per game. Once a player's NFL game kicks off, he is locked for the rest of the scoring week.
- A locked player cannot be moved between slots, benched, started, DROPPED, traded, or moved to or from IR until the next scoring week begins. Only the league manager can override.
- Thursday, Friday, or Saturday games lock those players early. Free agents lock too: a free agent whose game has started can't be added until the week rolls over.
- The scoring week rolls over after the Monday night game; players unlock about 2 AM CT Tuesday (ESPN's pages disagree on the exact hour, so trust each player's lock flag). After the rollover, every unrostered player sits on waivers until the next run. Before naming any drop or swap, check rosterLocked and lineupLocked on the player's mRoster entry and propose only unlocked players.
- A bench player whose game has started can no longer be moved into the lineup that week. When a starter plays later in the week than his backup (for example, a Monday night starter with a Sunday backup), decide before the backup's kickoff.

## Waivers and free agents
- Traditional waiver priority, not FAAB (no budget bidding). A successful claim sends that team to the back of the order; a free-agent add costs nothing.
- The waiver order resets every week to the reverse of the standings (worst record picks first). At 1-5 we get high priority back each week, so a claim costs us little.
- Waiver period is 24 hours. A dropped player sits on waivers until the first waiver run at least 24 hours after the drop. Runs happen overnight, about 2:00 to 4:00 AM CT, every day except Tuesday. The Wednesday run is the big one, when most players clear after the weekend.
- A player who is added and dropped on the same day goes straight back to free agency, not waivers.
- No limit on adds or lineup moves.
- ESPN's transaction log may say "dropped to waivers" for a player who is actually a free agent. Check the Players page.
- Some players are on ESPN's undroppable list and cannot be dropped.

## IR
- One IR slot. Only players ESPN tags OUT or IR can go into it. Suspended players are NOT IR-eligible (ESPN). Treat Commissioner's Exempt players the same way (our rule; ESPN's help pages don't address it).
- If an IR player's tag changes to questionable or doubtful, he may stay in IR. If he loses his tag entirely, the roster becomes invalid and no adds, claims, or trades are allowed until he is moved out. A claim made while IR was legal can still fail if we activate the IR player and run out of roster spots.

## Trades
- Deadline is Wed Dec 2 2026 at 11:00 AM CT. Four veto votes block a trade, with a 24-hour review period. The trade must be accepted and the review finished before the deadline. Traded players arrive on the bench. Adds and drops continue after the deadline.

## Basic game rules that apply every week
- A player scores only for the week's NFL games. A player on a bye, OUT, or inactive scores zero in a starting slot.
- ESPN's status tag decides availability for our calls (Ken, Oct 15 2026). An official can't-play list (inactives, IR, PUP, suspension, Commissioner's Exempt) can bench a player before ESPN catches up, but never starts one.
- NFL practice (not an ESPN rule): game-day inactive lists come out 90 minutes before each kickoff, and final injury designations (out, doubtful, questionable) come out two days before a game, Friday for Sunday games and Saturday for Monday games.
- A team's D/ST scores on points and yards allowed plus sacks, takeaways, and return or defensive touchdowns. A kicker scores on field goals and extra points. Both depend heavily on the matchup, so both are usually picked week to week.
- The weekly score is the sum of the nine starters only. Bench points count for nothing.

## Checklist before any recommendation
1. Is every starting slot filled with a player who has a game this week (no bye, no OUT)?
2. Is every player I want to move, drop, or trade unlocked right now?
3. If I'm adding someone, is he a free agent (instant, free) or on waivers (claim runs overnight and costs priority)?
4. Is anyone I want to put on IR tagged OUT or IR on ESPN?
5. Does a later-week starter have a backup who plays earlier and would lock first?
6. Does the add break a position cap (TE is full)?
7. Where does my drop go? Usually to waivers for at least 24 hours, where other teams can claim him.
8. For a trade: is it before Wed Dec 2, 11:00 AM CT, with time for the 24-hour review?
