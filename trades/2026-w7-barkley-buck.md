## The offer and how it got here

## What we get

Barkley projects about 13.1 a game for the rest of the season. He has scored only 23 points in four games and missed the last two with a hamstring injury. He was limited in practice Thursday and Friday (Eagles report, Fri Oct 23), ESPN tags him QUESTIONABLE, and he hopes to play Monday night against Dallas. The Eagles are on their backup quarterback, Tanner McKee, because Jalen Hurts is on injured reserve and expected to miss the season, and DeVonta Smith is expected to miss Monday's game. The case for Barkley is his ceiling once healthy, not his recent output. His bye is Week 10.

## What we lose

- Gordon projects about 12.4 a game and is our best running back by rest-of-season projection after Jacobs. His bye is done.
- Meyers projects about 10.9 a game. He is our fourth receiver and the fill-in when a starting receiver is on bye. His bye is this week.
- Barkley over Gordon is a small projected gain, and we also give up Meyers. On projection alone this deal does not clear the "never trade a starter for a small gain" bar. Ken has chosen to keep it for Barkley's upside in must-win mode.

## Where we fill in

- Wide receiver: without Meyers we have London (bye Week 11), Wilson (bye Week 13), and Bernard (bye Week 9). That leaves two healthy receivers in Weeks 9, 11 and 13, with nobody for FLEX from the receiver group. See the pickup plan below.
- Running back: the group becomes Barkley, Jacobs, Marks and Henderson (plus Harvey until the Goff trade). In Week 10 Barkley is off; in Week 11 Jacobs and Henderson are off; Marks is off in Week 8.
- Roster: two for one opens a roster spot, so no drop is needed to take Barkley.

## What Buck up Buttercup! gets

Buck is 2-4. Gordon gives them a fourth healthy running back and covers their bye weeks: McCaffrey is off in Week 8, and Irving and Dobbins are both off in Week 10. Meyers projects about the same as their starting receivers, Jameson Williams and Jordan Addison, and adds depth behind them with Rashee Rice out. Both players are healthy and have their byes behind them or this week. They have exactly enough bench room to take two for one without a drop.

## What Buck up Buttercup! loses

Barkley sits on their bench right now, so they lose no current starter. The real loss is his upside once his hamstring heals.

## Why Buck would say yes

Two healthy, usable players for one injured bench running back who has scored little this year, and the trade fills their Week 8 and Week 10 running back holes. It is a fair offer, not a lopsided one.
