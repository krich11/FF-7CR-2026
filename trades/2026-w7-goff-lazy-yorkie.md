# Goff trade with Lazy Yorkie (team 7): decision

Written Fri Oct 23 2026, 1:15 PM CT, Week 7. Corrected Sat Oct 24 2026: removed a wrong claim that Goff covers a Murray Week 14 bye (Murray is with Minnesota, whose bye was Week 6), and replaced the Wednesday Andrews claim with the free-agent add plan. Decision: yes. Offer Hunter Henry and RJ Harvey for Jared Goff on Tuesday Oct 27, once Henry unlocks. Do not go higher, and do not include a starter.

## What we get
Goff averages 23.0 points over five games. ESPN projects him at 18.5 a game for the rest of the season, about 1.6 a game above Kyler Murray (16.9). Both quarterbacks have already had their byes: Murray plays for Minnesota and Goff for Detroit, and both teams were off in Week 6. So the gain is not bye coverage. The true gain is four things: a better projected starter for the rest of the season, a second starting-caliber quarterback in case Murray leaves another game early, a real backup in Week 8 when McCarthy (Giants) is on bye, and a two-for-one that opens the roster spot Mark Andrews needs. We give up no starter to get it.

## What we lose
Henry (9.7 a game) is a bench tight end behind Pitts and Goedert. Harvey (10.7) is a bench running back and our fill-in if Jacobs sits. Neither is a starter.

## Where we fill in
Tight end stays covered by Pitts and Goedert. Once Henry leaves we drop to two tight ends, so Andrews can be added as a free agent right away (or claimed in Thursday's waiver run if he has moved to waivers); see playbook/CLAIMS.md. Running back depth becomes Jacobs, Marks, Gordon and Henderson. If the Barkley offer also goes through, Gordon leaves and Barkley joins, so the group becomes Barkley, Jacobs, Marks and Henderson. With Goff, Murray and McCarthy, we would carry three quarterbacks, so McCarthy becomes the next drop.

## What Lazy Yorkie gets
Henry upgrades their starting tight end, Cade Otton (6.3), by about 3 points a game. Harvey upgrades a running back group where Allgeier (6.7) and Emanuel Wilson (9.4) start. Both are healthy and would start for them right away.

## What Lazy Yorkie loses
Goff is their bench quarterback behind Aaron Rodgers (16.5 average, 19.5 projected this week), so he scores them nothing while he sits. The real loss is their insurance for Rodgers.

## Where Lazy Yorkie fills in
Rodgers has a bye in Week 9 (Pittsburgh), so they would need a quarterback that week. Geno Smith, Michael Penix and Jacoby Brissett are free agents projecting 14 to 16 points. To take two players for one, they must drop one. Brandin Cooks (3.3) or their second kicker, Tyler Bass, is an easy cut.

## Why they might say no
Goff has outscored Rodgers this year, so they may decide to start him instead of trading him. This is a fair offer, but not a sure one.

## Rejected versions
- Goedert alone, or Harvey and Goedert: Goedert is questionable, so this reads as a damaged backup for a starting quarterback (Ken, 1:10 PM).
- Garrett Wilson for Goff: fair to them, but bad for us, because we would give up our best receiver for a quarterback projected only slightly better than Murray.
