# Trade evaluation: Henry + Harvey to Lazy Yorkie for Jared Goff

Written by Assistant, Fri Oct 23 2026, 1:15 PM CT. Data: ESPN league 1776545061, read only, pulled today.

## Decision

Yes. Offer Hunter Henry and RJ Harvey for Jared Goff on Tuesday, Oct 27, after Henry unlocks. Do not go higher. If Yorkie declines, walk away.

## What we get

Jared Goff (DET, bye already taken). He averages 23 points. ESPN projects him about 3 points a week better than Murray for the rest of the season. He also covers Murray's Week 14 bye.

## What we lose

- Hunter Henry: our third TE, behind Pitts and Goedert. He is on bye in Week 11, the same week as Pitts, so he doesn't help us that week anyway.
- RJ Harvey: a bench RB who averages 10.7. He is our second fill-in this week if Jacobs sits, behind Gordon.

## Where we fill in

- The trade is two for one, so it opens a roster spot. Use it Wednesday to claim a WR for Week 11.
- We would carry three QBs (Goff, Murray, McCarthy). Murray or McCarthy becomes trade material.
- If the Barkley trade also goes through, our RBs are Jacobs, Marks, Barkley and Henderson. That is enough.
- Week 10: Goedert and Harvey's teams are on bye. Pitts covers TE.

## What Yorkie gets

- Henry (avg 9.7) replaces Cade Otton (avg 6.3) as their starting TE.
- Harvey (avg 10.7) replaces Tyler Allgeier (avg 6.7) as a starting RB.
- Their two weakest starting spots both get better every week.

## What Yorkie loses

Goff, who sits on their bench behind Aaron Rodgers (avg 16.5). Goff has been the better QB, so the cost to them is real but modest.

## Where Yorkie fills in

- Week 9: Rodgers' bye. Free agents Geno Smith, Michael Penix Jr. and Jacoby Brissett are available today.
- They must drop one player to make room. Brandin Cooks (avg 3.3) or their second kicker, Tyler Bass, is an easy cut.
- Week 10: Harvey's bye. Week 11: Henry's bye.

## Why it is fair

We get a small weekly edge at QB plus late-season QB cover. They get two starters at their weakest positions. Neither player we send starts for us.

## Rejected versions

- Goedert alone, or Goedert and Harvey: Goedert is questionable for Monday and is our second TE. That is a weak offer.
- Garrett Wilson for Goff: fair for Yorkie but bad for us. Wilson is our best WR, and Goff's edge over Murray is only about 3 points a week.

## Standing checklist for every trade

1. What we get, and what it adds to our starting lineup each week.
2. What we lose, and who replaces it in our lineup.
3. What they get, and whether it starts for them.
4. What they lose, and who replaces it in their lineup.
5. Byes and injuries on both sides for the rest of the season.
6. Fill-ins both teams will need from free agency, and who is available.
7. Locks: no player whose game has started that week can move.
8. Decision: a fair deal for both sides, or no offer.
