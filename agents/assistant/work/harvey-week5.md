# RJ Harvey, Week 5 (DEN 23, LAC 17, Sun Oct 11 2026): why 0.7 PPR points

Written Sun Oct 11 2026, about 6:45 PM CT. Sources are listed at the bottom.

## What happened
- Harvey was active with no injury tag. He had 3 carries for 7 yards and 1 target with 0 catches, for 0.7 PPR points. No in-game injury was reported and he had no fumbles. (Bo Nix fumbled twice and lost one.)
- J.K. Dobbins started and took the work: 18 carries for 68 yards and a TD, plus 2 catches for 6 yards on 2 targets. Tyler Badie had 1 carry and 1 catch for -5 yards. Marvin Mims and Jaylen Waddle also had carries.
- This was Sean Payton's first game after taking back play-calling duties (Pat Bryant is on IR). Badie and Mims carried the ball before Harvey got his first touch, and Harvey had 2 carries for 6 yards at halftime (Hindustan Times).
- Payton after the game: "It's a different offense a little bit... you get burned a few times and you don't touch the stove again" (AP via CBS). That reads as a reaction to turnovers and a conservative, Dobbins-run approach. I found **no Payton quote explaining Harvey's usage** and no beat report of a demotion, injury or discipline as of Sunday evening.
