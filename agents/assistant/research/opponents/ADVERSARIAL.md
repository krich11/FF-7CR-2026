# QBZ adversarial review: attacking our own flattering conclusions
*Written Sun Oct 11, 2026, ~10 PM CT. Weeks 1–4 are final. Week 5 is shown only where marked and is still partial. Data comes from read-only ESPN pulls (see README, `draft_players.csv`, `transactions.csv`). Preseason news was checked on the web, and every source is cited.*

The earlier conclusions under attack: (a) our draft was about average, (b) injuries are the main cause of our low scoring, and (c) our moves are the best in the league.

## 1. Was the injury risk foreseeable? Verdict: **partly**
- Our draft was on Thu Sep 3 at about 9 PM CT.
- **Josh Jacobs**, drafted in round 8 at pick 85, had been put on the NFL commissioner's exempt list on Aug 30, four days before the draft ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/2026/08/30/josh-jacobs-commissioners-exempt-list-nfl-packers/91538596007/), [The Athletic](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7550624/2026/08/30/josh-jacobs-commissioner-exempt-list-packers-nfl/)). His ESPN Week 1 projection was **0.0**. He has played 0 of 5 games, and the reported outlook is a 4–6 game suspension after he comes off the list ([NBC](https://www.nbcsports.com/fantasy/football/news/2026-nfl-week-4-injury-report-achane-acl-baker-thumb-jefferson-ankle-breece-hall-quad-and-more)).
- **Zach Charbonnet**, drafted in round 15, was coming off ACL surgery from last season's playoffs and opened the season on PUP ([SI](https://www.si.com/betting/zach-charbonnet-injury-update-betting-impact-for-49ers-vs-seahawks-in-nfl-week-5-2026)). His Week 1 projection was **0.0**, and he has played 0 of 5 games.
- **Reclassification.** These 2 players account for **8 of our 18 missed games (44%)** and about **67 of the 207 points** credited earlier to injuries (valued at what other players drafted near the same picks scored). That is a draft error, not bad luck. Across the whole league, the other 11 teams drafted 4 players who were already ruled out like this, for 8 missed games combined (DDT 4, LL 2, LY 1, MBB 1). We matched that total by ourselves.
- **What survives.** Achane tore his ACL in Week 3 ([NBC](https://www.nbcsports.com/fantasy/football/news/2026-nfl-week-4-injury-report-achane-acl-baker-thumb-jefferson-ankle-breece-hall-quad-and-more)), and Dart, Goedert, Etienne, Brooks, Pittman and Darnold missed games during the season. Brooks's earlier ACL history was not re-checked here. These absences cost about 140 points. The league-average loss is 63, so our genuinely bad injury luck is about **+77 points over 4 weeks**, not 144.

## 6. Process failures. Verdict: **holds**
- **Dead bench spots** (a bench player projected for 0) took up **9 of 35 bench slot-weeks (26%)**:
  - Jacobs in all 5 weeks. As an exempt-list player he can't go on IR, so he has blocked a roster spot all season.
  - Pittman in Weeks 2 and 5.
  - Goedert in Weeks 3 and 4.
- **Roster churn:** Strange was added and dropped within 2 days. Allgeier was held for 2 weeks and never started.
- **Roster balance:** in Weeks 4 and 5 we carried **3 TEs and 2 QBs** but only **3 RBs who could actually play** (Jacobs is dead weight). That is why Week 6 needs Woody Marks.
- **Week 6 byes:** Gordon (MIA) and Murray (MIN) are on bye, Meyers (JAX) is off in Week 7, and Achane and Etienne are on IR. A Week 6 running-back crunch was predictable from the bye schedule plus the injury list.
- Not verifiable from ESPN data: when the Payton play-calling news broke, and tonight's Charbonnet stale-projection near-miss. Both are recorded here as process risks, not measured costs.

## 7. Were we unlucky in the schedule? Verdict: **refuted (the earlier claim does not hold)**
- Points against were **506.3, 2nd-highest** (league average 465.3, so +41).
- Against every team every week we'd be **12-32**, which implies **1.09 wins**. We actually have 1, so luck is **-0.09 wins**.
- We lose because we score **395.2 (11th)**, not because of our opponents.

## Honest grades
- **Draft: D.**
  - Two picks were already known to be out before the draft (Jacobs at 85, Charbonnet at 180).
  - Pitts (ADP 104) and Dart (ADP 120) were taken at 60 and 61, and Achane (ADP 45) at 12.
  - Rounds 1–8 came in -168 points against typical picks, and our players hit ESPN's projections at the worst rate in the league.
  - What went well: G. Wilson, London, Meyers, the Steelers D/ST and Darnold.
- **In-season management: C+.** Our adds still come out ahead overall (Brissett, Gordon, Murray), and our projection-match rate is middle of the pack. But we dropped Tucker and Allgeier, who kept scoring, the Strange/Henry swap went the wrong way, and the trades with LY were a wash.
- **Process: C-.** 26% of bench spots were dead, the roster was TE/QB-heavy while RBs were thin, and play-caller and injury news did not have a reliable trigger to act on.

## Three biggest self-inflicted mistakes (point cost)
1. **Reaching in rounds 1–8: about -168 points in Weeks 1–4** against the typical player drafted after each pick. The worst single picks were Pitts at 60 (11.7 against 73.0 for Davante Adams, who was still on the board) and Achane at 12 over Josh Allen.
2. **Drafting 2 players who were already ruled out (Jacobs, Charbonnet): about -67 points**, plus 5 weeks of a dead bench spot.
3. **Dropping players who kept scoring: about -51 points** (Tucker 24.2, Allgeier 21.4, Strange/Henry 5.7).

**What survives from the flattering version:** about 77 points of genuinely bad injury luck (Achane's ACL and others), net-positive pickups, and a lineup process that is mostly about average. The main cause, though, is a weak, overrated draft, made worse by avoidable picks of players who were already out.
